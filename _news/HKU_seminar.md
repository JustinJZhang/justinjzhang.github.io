#### AI-empowered Echocardiography: from Computational Imaging to VLM-based Reasoning

Speaker: Jiajing Zhang

Organiser: Wei-Ning Lee

Date: 24 Nov 2026 

Time: 5:15-6:15 pm

Venue: 603, Chow Yei Ching Building, The University of Hong Kong

---

Echocardiography captures fine cardiac structures and rapid heart motion in real time. Yet efficient acquisition and reliable interpretation remain constrained by the trade-off between frame rate and image quality, severe noise, and complex anatomy. This talk presents a research path toward AI-empowered echocardiography through computational synthetic-aperture ultrasound and vision-language model (VLM)-based image interpretation. To boost image quality at high frame rates, we introduce a spatiotemporal adversarial framework that recovers IQ data from sparse transmissions, followed by coherence-guided IQ signal denoising adaptable to individual acquisitions through test-time training. To facilitate anatomical understanding of echocardiograms leveraging medical or general foundation models, we then propose an image-formation-guided medical SAM and investigate standard view recognition using VLMs. Together, these studies outline a progression from reconstructing informative cardiac images from sparse and noisy signals to supporting precise clinical interpretation for next-generation ultrasound systems.
